# pk-wedding-

Royal Wedding Invitation Website for **Purushothaman & Kanchana** (Wednesday, 11th November 2026).

## Features
- Royal Sage & Emerald Green, Ivory, and Warm Gold Theme
- Interactive Audio Melody Synthesizer (Raag Mohanam Harmony)
- Live Countdown to Muhurtham
- Single 1-by-1 Royal Invitation Card & Auspicious Muhurtham details
- Dual-Language Welcome Greeting (English & Tamil)
- Left-and-Right Scrollable Moments Gallery Carousel with high-resolution lightbox
- Comprehensive 3-Stop Journey Navigator with interactive live map and driving directions
- Interactive Guestbook & Wall of Blessings
- Add to Google Calendar & Apple Calendar (.ics)
- WhatsApp Invitation Sharing
- Created by Srikanth & Loganathan
